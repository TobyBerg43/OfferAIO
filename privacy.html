Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

OfferAIO ("we", "us") provides a website at offeraio.com and a Chrome browser extension, "OfferAIO — Auto Apply", that helps you fill out Summer 2027 internship applications in your own browser. This policy explains what the extension and site collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. We built OfferAIO to keep your data on your device by default.

The short version:
  • The details you enter (your application profile) are stored locally in your browser, not on our servers.
  • The extension only runs on the job-application sites it supports and on the OfferAIO dashboard.
  • You review and submit every application. CAPTCHAs are never bypassed. Your resume is stored in your browser and attached to the employer's own form — it is never sent to OfferAIO.
  • If you ask for an AI cover letter, that one request — and only that request — leaves your device. Everything else stays local.
  • We never see your card details. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Information the extension stores

When you save your profile in the extension (or sync it from the OfferAIO dashboard), the following is stored on your device using the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Contact and application details you enter: name, email, phone, school, graduation date, major, minor, GPA, LinkedIn URL, work-authorization/sponsorship preference, and an optional cover-letter template.
  • Your apply-mode preference (semi-auto or full-auto).
  • A count of how many applications you have submitted this month, so the extension can show your remaining monthly allowance. It resets each month and is never sent anywhere.
  • If you are on Pro: your license key and a randomly generated install identifier.

This information stays in your browser. The extension never transmits your profile to OfferAIO servers — the only things it sends us are the license key and install identifier, and only to check that your subscription is active.

How the information is used

Your saved profile is used for a single purpose: to fill the fields of internship application forms on supported sites when you click "Fill", so you can review and submit them yourself. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Cover-letter generation (Pro)

AI cover letters are the one feature that leaves your device. When you request one, the company name, the role title, the profile details needed to write it, and any writing sample you have supplied are sent to our service (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards them to OpenAI to generate the draft. We do not store the request or the resulting letter after it is returned to you; OpenAI processes it as our API provider and does not use API content to train its models. Your resume file is never part of this — see below.

If you do not use this feature, none of your profile ever leaves your browser.

Licensing and payments (Pro)

Pro is billed through Stripe. We never receive or store your card number — Stripe handles checkout and sends us only what is needed to run the subscription.

  • When you subscribe we generate a license key and store it alongside your email address, subscription status, and billing-period end date, so we know whether the key is currently active.
  • To check that key is valid, the extension and dashboard send the key and a randomly generated install identifier to our service. The install identifier is not tied to your identity; it exists only so a single key can be limited to a small number of browsers.
  • We count how many AI cover letters a key has used in the current month, to enforce the monthly limit.

On the free plan none of this applies — there is no account, no key, and nothing to verify.

Your resume

If you save a resume in the extension, the file is stored in your own browser (chrome.storage.local) and nowhere else. When you fill an application, the extension attaches that file to the employer's own form, in your browser — so submitting the application sends it to that employer, exactly as it would if you had picked the file by hand. That is the only place it ever goes.

Your resume is never sent to OfferAIO, and never forms part of an AI cover-letter request. We have no server that receives it and no copy of it. Remove it at any time from the extension popup, which deletes it from your browser immediately.

Some employers use a custom uploader that will not accept a file placed this way. When that happens the extension highlights the field and tells you it could not attach it, rather than letting you submit an application you believe carries a resume.

Permissions the extension requests, and why

  • Storage — to save your application profile locally.
  • Active tab & scripting — to run the form-filler on the application page you are viewing, only when you click "Fill".
  • Access to specific job sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, Workable, Jobvite, BambooHR, Breezy, Taleo, Handshake, LinkedIn Jobs, ZipRecruiter, Indeed, Wellfound) — to fill application fields on those sites.
  • Access to offeraio.com — to let the OfferAIO dashboard sync your profile into the extension.
  • Access to offeraio-worker.tobybergerbusiness.workers.dev — our own service, used only to check whether a Pro license key is active and to request AI cover letters.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it with third parties only in these cases:

  • OpenAI — to generate a cover letter you asked for.
  • Stripe — to take payment and manage a Pro subscription.
  • Where required by law.

Your choices and data deletion

You can edit or clear your profile at any time in the extension. Removing the extension deletes its locally stored data from your browser, including any license key. If you cancel Pro, the key stops working at the end of the period you have paid for. To have the license record associated with your email deleted, or for any other help with your data, contact us below.

Children

OfferAIO is intended for college students and other adults seeking internships and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
