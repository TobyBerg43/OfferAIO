ON THE CHROME WEB STORE · ONE-CLICK INSTALL
1

Install the OfferAIO extension

OfferAIO runs as a Chrome extension so applications go out from your own browser, on your own logged-in sessions — never from our servers.

Add to Chrome — free
One click, no developer mode, and Chrome keeps it up to date. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.
Or load it manually from a .zip
Only needed if your browser blocks the store, or you want a build newer than the published one.
1 · download the zip and unzip it somewhere permanent — Chrome reloads it from that folder every launch
2 · open chrome://extensions · 3 · turn on Developer mode (top right)
4 · click Load unpacked · 5 · pick the unzipped folder

2

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, find OfferAIO, and hit the pin. You'll want it one click away on every application page.

What OfferAIO will never do

  • Submit without you — you approve every application (full-auto is opt-in and clearly labelled)
  • Bypass a CAPTCHA — if one appears, it's handed straight back to you
  • Send your resume anywhere but the employer's own form — it is stored in your browser and attached locally, never uploaded to us
  • Answer a work-authorization question it isn't sure about — it leaves it blank and tells you
  • Send your profile to our servers — it stays in your browser
